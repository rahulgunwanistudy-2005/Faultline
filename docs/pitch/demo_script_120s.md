# Exact Two-Minute Demo Script — v0.2.0

Target recording length: **1:55–1:59**. Keep the synthetic-data badge visible and do not describe the upload as live handwriting inference.

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook

**On screen:** Worksheet stack and teacher quote.

**Voice:**

> “A teacher wrote: ‘During the lessons they all seem to get it, but test day comes and most of them fail.’ A score shows who was wrong. It does not show the repeatable procedure producing the answers.”

## 0:10–0:22 — Product sentence

**On screen:** Faultline landing and upload boundary.

**Voice:**

> “Faultline maps those procedures—not a grade, and not an invented mastery prediction.”

## 0:22–0:39 — Verified prototype boundary

**On screen:** Upload a PNG worksheet; show validation/segmentation stages and the disclosure toast.

**Voice:**

> “This build verifies and segments one known worksheet template. Live handwriting recognition is not enabled yet, so the diagnosis view switches to a clearly labeled synthetic regression fixture. The core then executes every candidate procedure against each visible answer.”

## 0:39–0:54 — Class action map

**On screen:** Four populated action lanes.

**Voice:**

> “The same exit ticket produces different next moves: nine students show a stable wrong procedure, one shows a matched wording-versus-computation split, one is ready to extend, and one is withheld because the evidence is not strong enough.”

## 0:54–1:16 — Prediction-before-reveal proof

**On screen:** Open Bea; show posterior and evidence. Lock the held-out prediction, then reveal.

**Voice:**

> “For Bea, adding tops and bottoms separately reproduces every visible answer. Faultline now locks its prediction for an unseen problem: three tenths. Only after that signed token exists can the server reveal the held-out answer. It is three tenths—an exact match.”

## 1:16–1:32 — Ambiguity and information gain

**On screen:** Open Jai and the next-question tab.

**Voice:**

> “For Jai, Faultline refuses a named diagnosis. Because the candidate procedures are executable, it chooses the questions that separate the surviving explanations by exact expected information gain.”

## 1:32–1:45 — Evidence and safety

**On screen:** Evidence Lab and repository test output.

**Voice:**

> “The current 12 out of 12 result is only a deterministic fixture regression check. It is not handwriting accuracy and not classroom impact. Future model-proposed rules are restricted JSON and must pass the executable verifier.”

## 1:45–1:58 — Close

**On screen:** Tomorrow plan export and final mark.

**Voice:**

> “Faultline does not promise a made-up improvement percentage. It shows the evidence, tells the teacher when to re-explain, and knows when three more questions are safer than a confident guess. Find the procedure beneath the score.”
