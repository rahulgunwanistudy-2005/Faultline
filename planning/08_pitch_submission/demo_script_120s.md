# Exact Two-Minute Demo Script

Target recording length: **1:55–1:59**. Speak at a calm, deliberate pace. Captions must match the final recording.

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook

**On screen:** A real-looking stack of worksheets and the teacher quote.

**Voice:**

> “A teacher wrote: ‘During the lessons they all seem to get it, but test day comes and most of them fail.’ Scores tell her who was wrong. They do not tell her what procedure each student is actually repeating.”

## 0:10–0:22 — Product sentence

**On screen:** Faultline landing and upload.

**Voice:**

> “Faultline turns a stack of fraction work into a map of those procedures—not a grade, and not an invented mastery prediction.”

## 0:22–0:39 — Live flow

**On screen:** Upload demo stack; page crops and readings appear.

**Voice:**

> “A vision model transcribes the answers and intermediate steps. Low-confidence handwriting is reviewed instead of guessed. Then the deterministic core executes every candidate wrong procedure against the student’s exact problems.”

## 0:39–0:54 — Class action map

**On screen:** Three action lanes form.

**Voice:**

> “This class is not one failure. Seven students are adding tops and bottoms separately. Four have no stable procedure. Three solve bare fractions correctly but break on matched word problems. Those are three different Monday lessons.”

## 0:54–1:16 — Magic proof

**On screen:** Open Diego; show posterior and item evidence. Display held-out problem and prediction; then reveal crop.

**Voice:**

> “For Diego, Faultline’s leading rule reproduces six of seven answers. Now the real test: before seeing this held-out page, it predicts he will answer three tenths. The page says three tenths. That is why the diagnosis is more than an LLM explanation.”

## 1:16–1:32 — Ambiguity and information gain

**On screen:** Near-tied student and three diagnostic items.

**Voice:**

> “When two procedures fit, Faultline does not guess. Because the rules are executable, it chooses the three questions that split the remaining possibilities by exact information gain.”

## 1:32–1:45 — Trust and evaluation

**On screen:** Evidence Lab with labeled confusion matrix.

**Voice:**

> “We evaluate transcription, rule identification, and end-to-end controlled handwriting separately. The model may propose a new pattern, but a safe program verifier must reproduce the work before the teacher sees it.”

## 1:45–1:58 — Close

**On screen:** Tomorrow card and final mark.

**Voice:**

> “Faultline does not promise a made-up improvement percentage. It tells a teacher which idea to re-explain, which procedure to practice, and when she needs three more questions before deciding. Find the procedure beneath the score.”
